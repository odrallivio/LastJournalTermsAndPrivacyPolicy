Privacy Policy

Last Journal · Effective 15 September 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information the Last Journal app ("the app") and its optional cloud backup service handle, why, and what control you have. It is provided by Aldene Villardo ("we", "us").

The short version: your journal lives on your device. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your information. If you choose to back up, your journal and photos are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create — entries, tags, moods, templates, saved searches, photos and settings — is stored on your device. The app works fully without an account, and none of it reaches us unless you sign in and back up.

One thing to know that isn't us: if Android's own backup is switched on for your device, Android copies app data — including your entries and photos — to your personal Google Drive, the same way it does for your other apps. That happens between your device and Google; we never receive it and can't read it. On Android 9 and later it is end-to-end encrypted with your device's PIN, pattern or password. You can turn it off in your device's system settings, under Google backup.

  • Photos you attach are resized and re-saved on your device. This removes location and camera details (such as GPS coordinates) from the copy the app keeps. Your original photo in your gallery is not changed or uploaded. A photo you take inside the app is saved the same way, and the temporary file the camera creates is deleted once the photo has been added to your entry.
  • App lock uses your device's own fingerprint, face or PIN check. We never receive it.
  • Exports (RTF and PDF) are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

4. Encryption

Backups and photos are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, and stored only in encrypted form. We don't look at their contents.

  • By default, the encryption key is protected by your account, so backups work without anything extra to remember.
  • With an optional backup password, the key is protected by your password instead, and only someone with the password can read your backups — including us. We can't recover a forgotten password.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Google Play on your device; we don't keep a record of it on our servers.

8. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Backups: until you delete them, until they're replaced by newer ones under the backup limits, or until you delete your account.
  • Photos in cloud storage: until you remove them ("Free up space") or delete your account.
  • After a subscription ends, or if an account is inactive, stored data may eventually be removed as described in our Terms of Use, always with at least 30 days' notice by email.
  • Your account: until you delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account — your entries and photos stay on your device.
  • Delete backups in Backup Management.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This permanently deletes the backups, photos and encryption keys stored for your account, and then the account. It doesn't touch your device.
  • Ask us for a copy of the information we hold, a correction, or deletion by contacting lastjournal.contact@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days. If a request is unusually complex and needs longer, we'll tell you within those 30 days and take at most 60 days in total.

If you're in the EU, UK or European Economic Area: we process account and backup information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. Your information is stored in the United States.

If you're in California: we don't sell or share personal information, and you have the right to know, delete and correct the personal information we hold.

10. Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

We use encryption on your device, secure connections, and access-controlled cloud storage where each account can only reach its own data. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — which is why the optional backup password exists for anyone who wants their backups readable only by them.

If we ever add optional usage statistics to help us understand which features are used, they will be off unless you turn them on, will never include anything you write or any photo, can be turned off at any time, and we will update this policy before introducing them.
