We will give you at least 30 days' notice first, by email to your account's address, so you have time to restore them to your device, free up space, or subscribe again.

9. Encryption and backup passwords

Backups and photos are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded.

Entries still on your device are not affected and can be backed up again.

You're responsible for remembering your backup password.
