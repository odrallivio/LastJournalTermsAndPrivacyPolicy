Delete your Last Journal account

Last Journal, by Aldene Villardo

You can delete your Last Journal account and everything stored for it in the cloud, either from inside the app or by asking us directly. Both routes delete the same things.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Last Journal on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Account Management, then choose Account And Backup Files Deletion.
  4. Confirm. Your account and everything stored for it in the cloud are deleted straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot reach that screen, email lastjournal.contact@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, and say that you want your account deleted. We will confirm once it is done, and in any case within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Every cloud backup stored for your account
  • Every photo you have backed up
  • Your encryption keys
  • Your account itself, including the email address held for it

This cannot be undone, and it is not reversible by us either — once your encryption keys are gone, any copy of a backup that might still exist is unreadable.

What stays on your phone

Deleting your account does not touch the journal stored on your device. Your entries and photos stay exactly where they are, and the app keeps working without an account. To remove the journal from the device as well, delete the entries in the app or uninstall it.
