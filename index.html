How it works

Small enough to use every day.

Open, write a line, close. Tamsi keeps the friction out so the habit can stay.

Writing

One tap to write.

Tap Write and a fresh page is ready with the keyboard up. Or start from a photo, a template or a prompt.

  • Templates for the things you log often
  • A Write widget and a Quick Settings tile
  • Daily reminder at the time you choose

Widgets

Start from your home screen.

Write in one tap, open a template, or go straight to a saved search, without hunting for the app first.

  • Write, camera and gallery buttons in one widget
  • Your templates and saved searches, one tap away
  • Names stay hidden while app lock is on, unless you choose to show them

Your day, your way

Notes, to-dos, done and events.

Log the day the way it happened. Each entry can carry a mood and tags, and your week is one tap away.

  • To-dos you can tick off, with unfinished ones moved to today if you like
  • Search by words, #tags and moods, saved searches and Insights
  • On this day: what you wrote in past years

Photos

The photos that made the day.

Add photos to any entry, or share them in from your gallery and other apps.

  • Location and camera details are removed from the copy Tamsi keeps
  • Your original photos stay untouched in your gallery
  • Share photos from an entry to any app

Prompts

Stuck? Ask for a prompt.

A gentle question to get you going, like “What was quietly good about today?” Swap it or remove it.

  • One tap for another prompt
  • Turn any entry into a reusable template

Privacy

Locked, and backed up your way.

Your journal is yours. Here's what that means in practice.

Read the Privacy Policy

  • Lives on your phone

    No account needed. Everything works offline, and your entries stay on your phone unless you turn on backups.

  • Encrypted before upload

    Backups and photos are encrypted on your phone, then stored only in encrypted form. Add a backup password for even stronger encryption.

  • Locked with your fingerprint

    App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN check. We never receive it.

  • Easy to take with you

    Export to RTF, PDF or a portable ZIP any time, or import from Day One, Obsidian and Markdown.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Tamsi the same app as Last Journal?

Yes. Tamsi is Last Journal's new name. Your entries, backups and Premium carry over as they are; there's nothing to do.

Do I need an account?

No. Tamsi works fully without one. An account is only for encrypted cloud backups, and you can add it any time.

Can you read my backups?

Backups are encrypted on your phone before upload and stored only in encrypted form, and we don't look at their contents. For even stronger encryption, add a backup password. How encryption works

How do I move to a new phone?

Back up on the old phone, sign in the same way on the new one, and restore. Step by step
